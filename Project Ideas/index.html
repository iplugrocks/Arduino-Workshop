Dear Tech Enthusiasts,

iPLUG is gearing up to host a series of hands-on workshops on Microcontrollers, Embedded Systems, and the Internet of Things (IoT) this academic year.

While we are planning for our sessions, we want your input. We want to build something that interests you. Share with us some project ideas that you would love if brought to real life, or learn how to build during the sessions.

What qualifies as a project idea?
▸ Campus & Daily Utility : Practical solutions designed to solve everyday operational or lifestyle challenges around campus or hostels.
▸ Automation : Systems designed to automate tasks or monitor environments.
▸ Robotics : Hardware-driven projects involving movement, navigation, motor control, or mechanical actuation.
▸ Internet of Things : Devices that gather data, interact with cloud platforms, or allow remote control over a network.
▸ Solving DIY & Research Challenges : Ideas addressing real-world technical hurdles researchers face, such as power management, sensor calibration, or signal noise.

Feel free to share any relevant idea even if it doesn't strictly fall into these categories.
